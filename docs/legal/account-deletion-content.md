# Delete your MyAmbii account

MyAmbii is operated by Mukaab Technologies Private Limited. This page explains how to
delete your MyAmbii candidate account and what happens to your information when you do.

## Delete from the app or the website

1. Sign in to the MyAmbii app, or to www.myambii.com
2. Go to Profile → Account
3. Choose Delete account and confirm

Your account is closed immediately. You will be signed out and will not be able to sign in
again with the same phone number or email address.

## If you cannot sign in

Email privacy@myambii.com from the email address on your account, or include the phone
number you signed up with, and ask us to delete your account. We will verify that the
request comes from the account holder before acting on it, and complete the deletion within
30 days.

## What is deleted

- Your name, email address and phone number
- Your profile — headline, location, skills, work experience, education and any other
  details you entered
- Your profile photo
- Your uploaded resume
- Your connected Google and GitHub sign-in links
- The identifying details on any certification you added — its name, issuer, credential ID
  or link, and the uploaded file itself (see below for what stays)

After deletion, nothing in your account identifies you, and your profile cannot be found by
any employer on MyAmbii.

## What is kept, and why

Some records are kept, with your identifying details removed — they are no longer linked to
your name, contact details or profile, and cannot be used to identify or contact you.

- **Assessment attempts, scores and badges.** An employer who saw a verified badge during
  hiring relies on it being a real record. Keeping the result without the person attached
  preserves the integrity of the verification without keeping you identifiable.
- **Certifications.** The uploaded file is deleted and your name is removed, but a record
  that you held a verified certification — issuer, status, dates, skill — stays, for the
  same reason as a badge above.
- **Applications you submitted.** Employer records that already exist because you applied,
  were shortlisted, or interviewed are kept, anonymised — deleting your account doesn't
  create gaps in another organisation's hiring history. Your name and contact details are
  removed from them.
- **A record that the deletion happened**, with its date. This is the evidence that we acted
  on your request.
- **Billing and tax records.** If you have ever made a payment to us, we keep the billing
  details you provided — name, email address, phone number, billing address, and GSTIN where
  you gave one — along with the invoice and payment records. Indian tax law requires invoice
  and tax records to be retained. We do not currently delete these after a fixed period.

## Timing

Deletion from the app takes effect immediately. Emailed requests are completed within 30
days. Backups are kept on a rolling 7-day cycle, so a copy of your information may persist
in a backup for up to 7 days after deletion before it is overwritten.
