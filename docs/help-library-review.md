### What does my match score mean?

_Also matches:_ how is my match score calculated · why is my score low · what affects my match · how does matching work

Your score is based only on verified information: your verified skills and their levels, Verified certifications tagged to skills the job asks for, and your years of experience. A verified skill at or above the level asked for gets full credit. A verified skill below the level gets partial credit scaled by how close you are. A skill you've claimed but not verified gets limited credit. Required skills count double compared to nice-to-have skills. Your headline, role title, and location are for display and filtering only and don't affect the score.
