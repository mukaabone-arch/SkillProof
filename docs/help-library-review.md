### How does AI sourcing work?

_Also matches:_ how do I use AI sourcing · what is AI sourcing · how does the AI sourcing feature work · explain AI sourcing · AI sourcing explained

AI sourcing finds candidates for one of your live job postings and writes each of them a personal message. Nothing is sent until you approve it. You pick a live job, MyAmbii ranks candidates who are open to recruiters by their verified skills against the job, you review the drafted messages and edit them if you want, then send. Candidates reply with one tap: Interested, Not now, or Not for me.
