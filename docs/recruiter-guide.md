# MyAmbii recruiter guide

How MyAmbii works for recruiters, staffing companies and recruitment agencies placing candidates with their clients.

## Getting started {#getting-started}

Recruiters sign in at **myambii.com/staffing** (or **For business → Recruiter** on the home page) with a work email and a one-time code. There's no password.

The first time you sign in, you set up your organisation. Accounts created from the recruiter sign-in are set up as recruiters automatically, so you go straight to the recruiter portal: **Find talent**, **Requests**, **Submissions** and **Clients**. Employer tools such as job posts and assessment requests aren't part of the recruiter portal.

Your organisation is reviewed before you can search candidates. Once it's verified, its type (recruiter or employer) can't be changed. If you set up under the wrong one, contact us before verification.

## Finding available talent {#find-talent}

**Find talent** shows only candidates who have:

- turned on **Open to recruiters** in their MyAmbii profile, and
- confirmed their availability in the **last 30 days**.

Candidates who haven't confirmed recently drop out of search automatically, so everyone you see has recently said they're available. Candidates get a reminder before their availability goes out of date.

You can filter by verified AI skills and skill level, start date, contract or permanent work, and expected rate. Profiles are anonymous: you see verified skills, availability and conditions, but **not** names or contact details. Those are shared only when the candidate says yes to you.

### Verified skills {#verified-skills}

Skills shown on MyAmbii come from MyAmbii assessments, not from what a candidate typed into a CV. A skill level is evidence of what the candidate actually demonstrated.

## Availability checks {#availability-checks}

To contact a candidate, send an **availability check** for a specific role (title, type of work and, optionally, rate and start date).

- The candidate sees which organisation is asking and for what role, and answers **Available** or **Not now**.
- If they answer **Available**, their **name, email and phone number** are shared with you on the **Requests** page.
- A check that isn't answered within **48 hours** expires.
- A candidate can choose not to be contacted by your organisation again.

To protect candidates from being flooded, there are weekly limits: up to **100 checks per organisation per week**, and a cap on how many checks any one candidate receives each week.

## Clients {#clients}

Add the companies you place candidates with on the **Clients** page. Add the client's website domain as well as its name, because both are used to recognise the same company:

- Names are compared without legal suffixes, so "Acme Pvt Ltd", "Acme Ltd" and "Acme Inc." count as the same client.
- A matching domain (for example acme.com) also counts as the same client.

This matching is what prevents a candidate being submitted to the same client twice, including by different recruiters.

## Submitting candidates to clients {#submissions}

Once a candidate has shared their contact details with you, you can ask to **submit them to a client**. Nothing is sent to the client by MyAmbii; this records that you represent the candidate there, with the candidate's consent.

1. Choose the candidate, the client and the role.
2. The candidate is asked to approve. They have **72 hours** to decide; after that the request expires.
3. If they approve, you represent them to that client, and **no other recruiter can submit them there**.

Track each submission on the **Submissions** page and keep its status up to date as it moves through interviews, offer and placement. The candidate sees the status too, and can withdraw.

### How long representation lasts {#representation}

- Representation lasts **30 days** from approval.
- It's held for as long as the submission is at **interviewing** or **offer**, so it doesn't run out mid-process.
- When you mark a candidate as **placed**, your placement at that client is protected for **180 days**.

### When a submission is blocked {#blocked-submissions}

You can't submit a candidate to a client when:

- another recruiter already represents them to that client, or
- the candidate is already in that company's own hiring process on MyAmbii (they applied directly or were shortlisted by the company).

You'll see the reason when you try. This protects candidates from duplicate submissions and protects whoever brought the candidate to the client first.

## FAQ {#faq}

**Why can't I see a candidate's name?**
Names and contact details are shared only after the candidate answers **Available** to your availability check.

**A candidate disappeared from my search. Why?**
Either they turned off **Open to recruiters**, or they haven't confirmed their availability in the last 30 days. Candidates you've already been in touch with stay on your **Requests** and **Submissions** pages.

**Can I use the employer tools as well?**
No. Recruiter and employer organisations have separate portals. If your company also hires for itself, set up a separate employer organisation from the **Employer** sign-in.

**Does MyAmbii send the candidate's CV to my client?**
No. MyAmbii records consent and representation; how you present the candidate to your client is up to you.

**Who do I contact with questions?**
Use the [contact page](/contact).
